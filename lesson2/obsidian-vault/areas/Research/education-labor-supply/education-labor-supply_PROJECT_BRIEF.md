# Project brief

**Question.** Does one more year of compulsory schooling change women's labour supply later in life?

**Setting.** Italy's 1962 reform made middle school compulsory (age 14). Cohorts born from 1949 were exposed.

**Design.** Regression discontinuity in birth cohort, plus a staggered rollout across provinces as a check.

**Outcomes.** Participation, hours worked, part-time status at ages 35 to 55.

**Data.** Synthetic labour force survey extract, 1993 to 2019. *All numbers in this vault are invented.*
