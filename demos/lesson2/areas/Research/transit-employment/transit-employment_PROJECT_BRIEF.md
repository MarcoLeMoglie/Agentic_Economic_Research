# Project Brief

**Question:** Can improved access to transit help employment? (Fictional lecture exercise.)

**Motivation:** Imagine a municipal service rollout that changes commuting possibilities.

**Hypothesis:** Shorter travel times could increase search opportunities. The simulated data do **not** establish a causal result.

**Contribution:** A sandbox to test an agent's memory, methods and provenance rules.

**Design:** Synthetic municipality-year panel with staggered fictional introductions of improved bus service; identification assumptions remain unverified.
