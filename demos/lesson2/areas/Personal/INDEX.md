# Personal

Personal projects and life administration, kept deliberately separate from research and teaching.

## Projects

_No projects yet._ Create one when a personal matter actually needs durable memory; the vault is not a diary.

## Adding a project

```bash
bash .infra/scripts/new-project.sh Personal <slug> "<Title>"
```

Then register it here and in [general/MEMORY.md](../../general/MEMORY.md).

## Privacy rules

- No passwords, account numbers, identity documents, medical details, or third-party personal data.
- Record where an important document lives and who owns it, never its sensitive contents.
- If a matter involves another person, store the minimum needed to act and nothing more.
- Anything that would be damaging if the vault leaked does not belong in the vault.
