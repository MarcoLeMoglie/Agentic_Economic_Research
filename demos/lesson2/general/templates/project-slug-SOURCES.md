# Sources and Provenance

Register every source before using it. Originals live in `raw/` and are never edited. A source that has not been acquired is recorded as `unavailable` — never replaced by a plausible citation.

Status vocabulary: `acquired` · `partial` · `unavailable` · `superseded`.

## Provenance rules

- Quote exactly and give a page/section locator; paraphrase everything else.
- Separate the source's finding from its interpretation and from your inference.
- Note conflicts between sources here or in the project's `CURRENT_STATE.md`.
- Mark synthetic documents `SYNTHETIC` in the first line of the original and in this table.
- Do not cite a source you have not opened.
